    Hey, you, you're finally awake

    Woah there traveler! Welcome to your very own Bag of Holding for all things TTRPG-related! This is a place for game masters and players to keep their character's inventory, character sheet, and spells in one place. This website is designed with D&D 5.5e in mind, but is completely customizable to whatever your usecase might be. Let's get you started... are you a player or a game master?
